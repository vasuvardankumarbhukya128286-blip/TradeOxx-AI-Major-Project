## 📌 About TradeOxx Ai

**TradeOxx Ai** is a next-generation portfolio intelligence and market analytics platform designed to make complex financial data intuitive, transparent, and actionable. 

Built for modern investors, traders, and learners, TradeOxx Ai combines real-time market data visualization with intelligent analysis to help users monitor performance, evaluate risks, and understand the driving forces behind price movements.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* **⚡ Command Center & Portfolio Intelligence**: Real-time overview of portfolio holdings, asset distribution, realized/unrealized profit & loss, and comprehensive equity curves.
* **🤖 TradeOxx AI Insights**: On-demand market intelligence assistant providing contextual analysis, asset summaries, and clear explanations of market movements.
* **📈 Interactive Market Analytics**: High-performance charting, candlestick views, multi-timeframe exploration, and live market trends.
* **🔍 Market Screener & Dynamic Watchlists**: Multi-asset tracking across stocks, ETFs, indices, and crypto with customizable watchlists and live metric monitoring.
* **📰 Financial News & Economic Calendar**: Real-time financial headlines and scheduled economic events to keep you ahead of market catalysts.
* **🎓 Learning & Knowledge Hub**: Interactive modules and resources designed to build financial intuition and market literacy.
* **🛡️ Risk & Performance Metrics**: Deep-dive analytics evaluating portfolio volatility, exposure, and risk-adjusted metrics.
